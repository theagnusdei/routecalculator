A large clinical trial testing a personalized mRNA cancer vaccine reduced the risk of disease recurrence in patients with melanoma by 42% compared to a standard treatment, STAT tells us. The results are an important advance because patients lived significantly longer without cancer returning. The vaccine may also usher in a new wave of treatments for other types of cancer, though experts are debating how broadly applicable the technology could be. Merck and Moderna announced its success in August, but details including the magnitude of benefit were being withheld until a scheduled presentation Saturday at the annual meeting of the European Society of Medical Oncology. But an oncology news website published some of the data on Thursday, breaking the conference embargo.
A U.S. judge granted a request by the Pharmaceutical Research & Manufacturers of America and three drug makers to intervene in a lawsuit over a government rebate pilot program, pivoting from a previous decision denying their involvement, Bloomberg Law notes. The order will allow the trade group, AbbVie, Bristol Myers Squibb, and Boehringer Ingelheim to step into a fresh legal battle over the 340B Rebate Model Pilot Program slated to go into effect Jan. 1. Hospitals filed a challenge earlier this month seeking to block the U.S. Health Resources & Services Administration from allowing drugmakers to provide rebates instead of upfront discounts for medicines purchased through the 340B Drug Discount Program.
