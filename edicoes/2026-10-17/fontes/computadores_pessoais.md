TL;DR: A grade A refurbished Dell Latitude 5420 with an 11th Gen i5, 8GB RAM, 256GB SSD, and Windows 11 Pro is $264.99 (MSRP $1,099). Limited inventory available.
A brand-new business laptop with Windows 11 Pro, Thunderbolt 4, and an 11th Gen Intel processor usually costs north of $800. But if you don’t mind a refurbished model, this near-mint Dell Latitude 5420 brings all of that down to $264.99 (MSRP $1,099). The only “catch” is that inventory is limited, so order ASAP.
An 11th Gen Core i5-1135G7 runs at 2.4GHz and turbo-boosts to 4.2GHz, so spreadsheets, browser tabs, and video calls don’t compete for processing power. RAM is 8GB DDR4-3200, and the board supports up to 32GB if you want to upgrade later.
Storage is a 256GB PCIe NVMe SSD. Boot times and application launches are fast, though heavy media libraries or large project folders may need an external drive.
A 14-inch Full HD anti-glare display keeps text sharp and readable without the glare that makes glossy screens hard to use near a window.
Two Thunderbolt 4 ports, two USB 3.2 ports, and HDMI 2.0 give enough connectivity for a monitor, a dock, and peripherals without running out of slots. Wi-Fi 6 and Bluetooth 5.0 are both included. At 2.9 lbs, the laptop travels easily. A 90-day parts and labor warranty comes with the unit.
