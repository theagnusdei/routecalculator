## Texto extraído

Wolf pack approach to wind and solar power
Researchers have developed a new way of managing electricity supplies that could help keep power grids stable as they become more dependent on wind and solar energy. Details of the algorithmic approach, which is based on the hunting behavior of a wolf pack, are reported in the International Journal of Critical Infrastructures.
The growing use of renewable energy is a challenge to power infrastructure because wind and sunlight are unpredictable. Electricity generation can rise and fall quickly, making it harder to balance supply with demand. This can cause changes in the grid's frequency, a measure of the stability of the electricity supply.
The study offers a new approach to managing these fluctuations. It estimates how much support renewable sources can reliably provide before distributing instructions to thermal power stations, telling them how much to increase or reduce their output.
In tests, the method reduced frequency fluctuations to 0.11 hertz. It also achieved a maximum of over 87% reduction in renewable energy fluctuations. It was able to work out the instructions in under 0.4 seconds.
The findings could help electricity networks incorporate more wind and solar power by making better use of existing power stations to compensate for unpredictable changes in renewable generation.
Publication details
