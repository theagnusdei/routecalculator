## Texto extraído

Proton has released its AI assistant as a standalone computer app, Lumo Desktop. The app can search for documents on your computer and retrieve information from connected services such as Google Drive and Proton Mail.
Through Connections, the assistant can also work with local folders, run commands, and retrieve content from the web. Users decide for themselves which actions Lumo is allowed to perform automatically, which require approval, and which should be blocked entirely.
Proton
Another new feature is called Schedules, which lets you automate recurring tasks such as compiling emails every week or regularly reviewing documents and other information.
Proton has also introduced Skills, reusable instructions that control how the assistant carries out specific tasks. According to the company, neither conversations nor information from connected apps is used to train AI models. Chat history is also protected by encryption designed to prevent even Proton itself from reading the content.
Lumo Desktop is available to download for Windows, Linux, and macOS. The program requires a Proton account.
