## Texto extraído

Following Jensen Huang’s resignation as Nvidia’s CEO last week, Trump told reporters on Monday that he’s open to seeing Huang return and that he’s not certain that the woman Huang reportedly had an affair with was in fact a spy.
Trump says it ‘certainly isn’t a bad idea’ for Jensen Huang to return to Nvidia
The president also said he doesn’t know if one of the women Huang allegedly had an affair with was a Chinese spy.
“I think having Jensen go back– a lot of people are asking Jensen, they think Jensen should go back. That certainly isn’t a bad idea.” Trump said he “would not mind” seeing Huang return as CEO, adding, “I threw out the name of Tim Cook, but I would say that Jensen’s reinstatement– I think he learned a big lesson, I guess maybe one lesson more important than another, but I would certainly not mind seeing that.” Trump has suggested Tim Cook step in as Nvidia’s CEO multiple times now.
When asked about reports that Alice Huang, one of the women Jensen Huang allegedly had an affair with, was a Chinese spy, the president repeatedly claimed he wasn’t sure if it were true. “I don’t know that she’s a spy at all, nobody’s proven that,” Trump said.
Trump also denied that Marco Rubio told him Alice Huang was a spy. “Usually what happens with that is that, any woman coming to the country that’s a beautiful woman that comes from China, they say she’s a spy,” Trump said. “I don’t know that she was a spy at all. I mean, I really don’t. I don’t know that, you know, Jensen obviously has two things going. He has a family situation, which is a rough deal, and what he wants to do is sell chips.”
Trump went on to say national security “was not put at risk” by his close relationship with Jensen Huang.
