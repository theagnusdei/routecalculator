## Texto extraído

Agile Robots has raised $500 million in Series F funding. This brings the company’s valuation to $8.5 billion and total funding raised to $2 billion.
Agile Robots builds and deploys a range of robots, including humanoids, mobile robots, robotic arms, and robotic hands. It also builds the software and AI models behind these robots. The company’s customers include leaders in the automotive, consumer electronics, healthcare, and service industries.
“Physical AI will fundamentally change how the world builds, moves and makes things,” CEO and Founder of Agile Robots Dr. Zhaopeng Chen said. “Our vision is intelligent robots working alongside people in factories and warehouses everywhere, and Agile Robots is determined to lead that shift. We bring physical AI out of the lab and into industrial production, across logistics, automotive, electronics and manufacturing. This funding allows us to accelerate our global expansion, strengthen our presence in key markets and build out production capacity.”
SoftBank Group led the Series F round, which also included NVIDIA, Hitachi Group (Hitachi and Hitachi Ventures) and T.Capital. Other investors spanned the automotive, artificial intelligence, digital and industrial technology sectors.
“We believe physical AI will transform industries. Agile Robots combines deep robotics expertise with the ability to execute at industrial scale, enabling it to bring physical AI into everyday operations,” said Dennis Chang, managing partner at SoftBank Investment Advisers. “We are pleased to continue partnering with Dr. Chen and the Agile Robots team as they advance breakthroughs in industrial-scale automation.”
Inside Agile Robots’ plans to grow
The company said it will use the latest funding to support increased production capacity and expansion into key markets. Agile Robots plans to strengthen its presence in the United States and accelerate the development of innovative robotics applications.
Agile Robots is one of more than 200 companies tracked in The Robot Report‘s humanoid robot database, which provides a snapshot of the market. The database tracks each developer’s robots, locomotion systems (wheeled or legged), commercial availability and headquarters location.
Agile Robots will also continue to build out production capacity, including in Germany. The company opened earlier in 2026 a production site at Fürstenfeldbruck, near Munich, Germany, to meet rising demand. The site has the capacity to produce up to 10,000 legged and wheeled humanoid robots annually.
Agile Robots was founded in 2018 as a spin-off from the German Aerospace Center (DLR), with the goal of applying world-class robotics research to real world industry. Since then, the company has deployed 20,000 industrial robots for customers in the automotive, consumer electronics, logistics and other industries.
Now, the company has a growing portfolio of subsidiaries. These include including the global AI robotics research platform, Franka Robotics, and industrial deployment specialist Krause Automation, recently acquired from thyssenkrupp AG. Agile Robots manufactures in-house, including in Germany.
