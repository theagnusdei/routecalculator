Insider Brief
- Quantum Art has appointed Mark Solomon as Head of U.S. Strategic Growth and Business Development and Tal Inbar as Senior Vice President of Business Development.
- Solomon will lead U.S. commercial development, focusing on government agencies, national laboratories, research institutions and enterprise partnerships.
- Inbar will oversee business development outside the United States, focusing on international partnerships, market expansion and commercial strategy.
PRESS RELEASE — Quantum Art, a developer of full-stack, fault tolerant quantum computers based on trapped-ion qubits and a proprietary, dynamically reconfigurable multi-core architecture, today announced two additions to its leadership team: Mark Solomon has joined as Head of U.S. Strategic Growth and Business Development, and Tal Inbar has joined as Senior Vice President of Business Development, leading efforts outside of the United States.
Mark Solomon Named Head of U.S. Strategic Growth and Business Development
Mr. Solomon brings more than 35 years of experience in engineering, product management, strategic business development, technology commercialization, and market development. Most recently, he served as Vice President of Worldwide Sales at IonQ, where he played a key role in developing the company’s relationships and strategic engagement with the U.S. Federal Government. He joined IonQ as Director of Quantum Sales before advancing to Vice President. Mr. Solomon also represented IonQ at prominent industry forums, including Q2B and a Quantum Industry Coalition briefing in Washington, D.C.
Prior to IonQ, Mr. Solomon spent 35 years at IBM in various engineering, product management, and business development leadership roles. His experience included managing strategic research alliances, developing industry partnerships, and overseeing intellectual property licensing initiatives for IBM Research. Throughout his tenure, he received multiple IBM corporate awards for his contributions.
In his new role at Quantum Art, Mr. Solomon will lead the company’s U.S. strategic business development initiatives, focusing on expanding its market presence, cultivating high-value partnerships, and identifying opportunities to accelerate the adoption and commercialization of Quantum Art’s technology. Drawing on his 12+ years of experience working with quantum technologies and the quantum ecosystem, he will spearhead engagement with government agencies, national laboratories, research institutions, and enterprise organizations, building strategic relationships and developing new pathways for collaboration, innovation, and long-term growth.
“I’ve spent most of my career helping emerging technologies move from the lab into real-world applications through strategic partnerships and collaboration. What immediately stood out to me about Quantum Art is how far the team has already advanced its technology and its potential to transform the industry. I’m excited to work alongside this incredibly talented team to build meaningful partnerships, expand the ecosystem, and create new opportunities to bring this revolutionary technology to organizations across the U.S.,” said Mark Solomon, Head of U.S. Strategic Growth and Business Development at Quantum Art.
Tal Inbar Named SVP of Business Development
Tal Inbar brings more than 25 years of executive experience in business development, corporate strategy, M&A, and international business growth across the cybersecurity, telecommunications, and enterprise technology sectors. Most recently, she served as VP of Business Development & Strategic Alliances at quantum key distribution and quantum networking company HEQA Security, where she led global business development, sales, and strategic partnerships, driving significant revenue growth and international market expansion. Prior to that, she served as SVP of Business Development & Operations and a member of the top management team at NSO Group, where she played a key role in scaling the company’s business. During her tenure, she led global strategic partnerships and corporate development initiatives, including M&A transactions exceeding $100 million, joint ventures, and international commercial agreements. Earlier in her career, she held business development leadership positions at InfoGin and other technology companies and began her career as a corporate and M&A lawyer. She holds an Executive MBA with honors from Bar-Ilan University and an LL.B. from Tel Aviv University.
In her new role as Senior Vice President of Business Development at Quantum Art, Ms. Inbar will lead the company’s global business development and commercial strategy outside of the United States, focusing on expanding its international presence, establishing strategic partnerships, developing new markets, and creating business opportunities to accelerate the commercialization and adoption of Quantum Art’s quantum computing technology.
“Throughout my career, I’ve been drawn to transformative technologies and the opportunities they create. When I entered the quantum space, I quickly fell in love with the technology, its possibilities, and the incredible potential it holds to reshape industries. What excites me about Quantum Art is the opportunity to help turn that potential into reality, building partnerships, opening new markets, and creating the commercial foundations for long-term growth. I’m thrilled to be joining this team at such a pivotal moment in the evolution of quantum computing,” said Tal Inbar, Senior Vice President of Business Development at Quantum Art.
“Quantum Art is moving from technical milestones to commercial scale, and that transition is being driven by leaders whom buyers and partners already trust. Mark Solomon has spent his career bringing quantum technology to U.S. federal customers, and Tal Inbar has led global partnerships in quantum security. Together with our teams, they will put our trapped-ion systems in the hands of more customers around the world,” said Tal David, Co-Founder and CEO of Quantum Art.
